## Looks at

The progress a person is trying to make in a specific situation — the job — and what they "hire" today to get it done, including workarounds and doing nothing.

## Key questions

- Who is struggling, in what situation, and what progress do they want? (When … I want to … so I can …)
- What do they use today, and why is it not good enough? What makes them switch, and what holds them back?
- Is there more than one job or more than one person (buyer vs. user, creator vs. viewer)?
- Which functional, emotional, and social parts of the job matter most?

## Common gaps

- The plan describes features but not the situation that triggers the need.
- The buyer and the user are mixed up, so success is measured for the wrong person.
- The current workaround is ignored, so the plan cannot say why anyone would switch.

## Turning gaps into questions

Ask about the situation, not the feature. Offer job statements built from the context as options and recommend the one the request implies.
Example: "이 서비스를 가장 먼저 쓰는 사람은? ① 혼자 채널을 운영하는 1인 크리에이터(추천 — 요청이 '내 채널' 중심) ② 여러 채널을 맡은 대행사 담당자 ③ 브랜드 마케터 — 대상에 따라 승인 흐름과 화면이 달라져요."
When the user is the only user (an internal tool), assume the job from the purpose ladder and skip the question.

## Tensions

- With **purpose ladder**: the ladder holds the requester's goal, JTBD holds the customer's job. If they conflict (e.g. revenue vs. user effort), ask which one wins in the first release.
- With **MoSCoW**: a full job needs many steps; cutting scope can leave the job half done — ask which step of the job the first release must finish end to end.

## When not to use

L0 documents and internal one-off tasks where the requester is the only user and the job is the request itself.

## Output form

Form: job statements — "<situation>에서 <누구>는 <진행>을 원한다, 그래야 <결과>" — with the current workaround and switching triggers.
Link to first layer: each answered job question becomes one statement; workarounds become the alternatives in the decision record.
When to produce: L2–L3 plans with users other than the requester; one line inside the purpose for L1.

## Closing

Pick the primary job and say what the plan does for it that the current workaround does not; other jobs become later scope or out of scope.
