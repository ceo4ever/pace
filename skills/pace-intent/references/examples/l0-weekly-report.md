## Request

"팀장에게 보낼 주간 보고 써 줘" (variant: with this week's items listed in the request).

## Lenses

purpose-ladder → audience ("보고", "팀장") → pyramid-scqa (report with a conclusion) → doc-skeletons (report must-haves). Not needed: jtbd, assumption-map beyond one `가정` block.

## Gaps that must surface

- reader's required action (share only vs. decision request) — assume "share only"
- the key message (top line) — derive from the facts given
- this week's facts — only the user has them

## Good and bad questions

- Bad: asking reader, tone, length, and format one by one; appending "있으면 같이 적어 주세요" lists after the question (D15).
